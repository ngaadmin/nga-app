# NextGenAchievers App Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## 1. Who we are
The NextGenAchievers app teaches kids money skills. It is run by Next Gen Achievers Pty Ltd (ACN 685 561 344, ABN 49 685 561 344), trading as NextGenAchievers ("we", "us"). We handle personal information in line with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This policy covers the app only. Our website has its own policy.

## 2. What we collect

| Who | What we collect |
|---|---|
| **Students (school class seats)** | A username created by the teacher; a placeholder email (not a real address); the shared class password; a placeholder birth year (not a real date of birth); role, learning track and link to their teacher; progress (lessons completed, XP/points, skills, Vault jars and goals, streak); usage activity events linked to the username (for example, lessons opened or completed). |
| **Students (parent accounts)** | A username; a placeholder email (not a real address); a stand-in birth year (not a real date of birth); the link to the parent's account; role and learning track; progress and usage activity events, as above. |
| **Parents and guardians** | Email address; the link between the parent and child accounts; guardian email addresses on consent requests. |
| **Teachers** | Email address; password (stored as a hash); a generated username (not a name); seat limit, learning track and the shared class password; the classes and student seats the teacher creates. We do not collect teachers' names or school names. |

We do **not** collect students' real names, full dates of birth or home addresses. We do **not** store IP addresses. They are used briefly, in memory only, to limit misuse (rate-limiting).

## 3. How we collect it
- From teachers, when they set up a class and create student seats.
- From parents, when they create an account, link a child or respond to a consent request.
- From the app itself, as students complete lessons and activities.

## 4. Why we use it
We use this information to run the app: to sign users in, show lessons, save progress, show teachers and parents the right information, send parents account and consent emails, keep the app secure, give support, and improve lessons using usage activity. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it for marketing.

## 5. Who can see it
- **Teachers** can see their own class only: student usernames, each student's lifetime XP and the class total.
- **Parents** can see their linked child's username, account status and learning track.
- **Authorised NextGenAchievers staff** can access stored data only to provide support and keep the service running (for example, resetting a password). The app has no feature that lets staff log in as a user.
- **Students** cannot see other real students. Any leaderboard shows sample names only.
- We do not share information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## 6. Service providers and overseas hosting
We use trusted providers to run the app. Some of them store or process data outside Australia:
- **Vercel** (app hosting): app functions run in the **United States** (Washington DC).
- **Supabase** (database and sign-in): data is stored in **Japan** (Tokyo).
- **Resend** (email): sends emails to parents only, from mail.nextgenachievers.com. [Region: TO CONFIRM]

We use no other third-party services in the app. That means no error monitoring, analytics, Google Fonts or third-party images.

Before information goes overseas, we take reasonable steps (including contract terms) to make sure these providers handle it consistently with the APPs (APP 8). We remain responsible for how they handle it.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage
The app uses a sign-in session cookie, plus a short-lived cookie during password recovery. It also stores a list of accounts used on the device, a progress cache and the session in your browser's local storage. We use **no** analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## 8. Children and schools
The app is designed for children, so we collect as little as we can. In schools, the teacher creates usernames that should not include a child's real name. The school keeps any list that matches usernames to students. Outside school, a parent or guardian sets up and consents to their child's account. We plan to follow the OAIC Children's Online Privacy Code once it is registered.

## 9. How long we keep it and how to delete it
We keep information while the account or class is active. Inactive accounts are deleted after 12 months of inactivity.
- Teachers can delete a single student or a whole class in the app. This removes their logins and progress.
- Deleting a parent account also deletes all linked child accounts.
- For any other deletion, including a single child's account, email us (see section 12).

Deleted data is removed from our live database; we don't keep restorable backups.

## 10. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access is limited by account role, and only authorised staff can reach stored data. Password reset events record the user ID and role, never the password. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the OAIC under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

## 11. Access, correction and complaints
You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you or your child. Email us, and we'll respond within 30 days. If you have a privacy complaint, email us first and we will reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
