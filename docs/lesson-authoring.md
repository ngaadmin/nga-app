## Error text

Every screen that can cost a heart needs Error text for all three cohorts: Explorer, Pathfinder, and Maverick.

A heart drops when the learner misses: a wrong choice, the wrong True or False, a wrong word in a blank, a wrong pick on a round, a drop in the wrong bucket, a card on the wrong step, a mismatched pair, a wrong order on Submit, letting go of a hold early, letting go off a drag target, a drop outside the savings box, letting go of the slider on an amount other than the target, or checking an item that is not needed or goes over the total.

These do not cost a heart, so they do not need an Error line: story and continue cards, free choices with no wrong answer, tap-reveal, and the skill hub.

The player shows that Error line when the heart drops. A blank cell leaves the miss with no sentence. New lessons cannot ship a blank Error cell on a heart-costing screen. Each cohort has its own cell. Leaving one blank does not count as written, even when another cohort already has a line.
