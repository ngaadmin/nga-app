# NextGenAchievers app privacy policy

Published October 30, 2026

## 1. Who we are
The NextGenAchievers app teaches kids money skills. It is run by Next Gen Achievers Pty Ltd (ACN 685 561 344, ABN 49 685 561 344), trading as NextGenAchievers ("we", "us"). We aim to follow the privacy laws that apply where our users live, and to take extra care with children's information. This page gives the details for the app. It's part of our [Privacy policy](https://nextgenachievers.com/privacy), which covers the app and our website and forms part of our [Terms and Conditions of Service](https://nextgenachievers.com/terms).

Our sign-in provider keeps the IP address and device or browser details for each active sign-in until the user signs out, the session is revoked, or the account is deleted. Our hosting and service providers also keep short-term technical logs, which may include IP addresses.

## 3. How we collect it

- From teachers, when they set up a class for their school or organization and create usernames.
- From Parent account holders, when they create an account, link a child or respond to a consent request.
- From learners who sign up themselves.
- From the app itself, as students complete lessons and activities.

## 4. Why we use it
We use personal information only to run NextGenAchievers:

- **To create and secure accounts.** We use a username and password to let you sign in and keep each account safe. The learning track (Explorer, Pathfinder or Maverick) sets the right lessons for each child.
- **To save your learning.** We store lesson progress, coins, Lifetime XP, medals and Vault activity so you can pick up where you left off. Coins are what a learner earns and cashes in. Lifetime XP is a separate progress score, and cashing in does not lower it.
- **To run school classes.** Teachers can see the usernames and Lifetime XP in their own class, and the class total.
- **To link parents and children.** We record which parent is linked to which child so parents can give consent, manage their household, change a child's learning track, recover usernames and delete accounts.
- **To send essential emails.** These are parent approval and consent emails, welcome emails, teacher class confirmations, password and username recovery, account-deleted notices, and the inactivity warning at 11 months. They go to Parent account holders, Self sign-up account holders and Adult account holders, and to teachers about their classes.
- **Optional emails.** Optional emails from us are only sent to a Parent account holder who ticks the box for them at sign-up. You can unsubscribe any time.
- **To show progress and keep accounts tidy.** For every signed-in learner, we record simple activity: signing in, starting, finishing or leaving a lesson, earning medals and opening Vault or Launchpad, together with the username, class (if any), Lifetime XP and streak. We use it to show the learner's progress, to show parents and teachers an activity list and best streak, to work out when an account was last used (section 9), for the leaderboard when it's switched on, and to report to schools on how classes use the app, for example school-hours versus home use. It doesn't include email addresses, real names, IP addresses or device details, and it stays in our own database. We don't use it for advertising or share it with anyone else.
- **To count guest play.** We store daily totals only: the day, event type, lesson, app version, device type and country. There is no username, and we do not store the IP address or the user agent.

We don't show ads, we don't use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share personal information. Our service providers handle data only to run the app: they store it, send emails and host the app.

We may use what learners add in the app, such as Vault jar names and goals, without names attached, to improve NextGenAchievers. When an account is deleted, this stops, except for information that no longer identifies anyone and has already been used to improve NextGenAchievers, and anything we must keep by law.

If any part of the app uses AI, please don't type real names, addresses or other personal information into it.

## 5. Who can see it

- **Teachers** can see one class only: usernames, each student's Lifetime XP and the class total. On a student's data page a teacher can also see that student's lessons, coins, Lifetime XP, medals, Vault jars and goals, streak and recent activity.
- **Parents**, behind the parent PIN, can see a linked child's lessons, coins, Lifetime XP, medals, Vault jars and goals, streak and recent activity, as well as the username and learning track.
- **Authorized NextGenAchievers staff** can access stored data only to provide support and keep the service running (for example, resetting a password).
- The **Community leaderboard** is off unless a parent switches it on for a child, or a teacher switches it on for a class. When it is on, it shows the signed-in user's username and sample player names, each with a rank, a day streak and Lifetime XP. It does not show other students, and nothing on it links to a profile.
- We do not share information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## 6. Service providers
We use trusted third-party providers for our database, sign-in, app hosting and email. They keep data securely and have their own requirements for storing it in line with global regulatory requirements.

Our providers may store or process your information in countries outside your own. Where the law requires it, we protect these transfers with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, and you can ask us for a copy. You can see our current list of service providers, what they do and where they are at [nextgenachievers.com/privacy/providers](https://nextgenachievers.com/privacy/providers).

Our email provider sends emails to Parent account holders, teachers, and Self sign-up and Adult account holders who give a personal email, from mail.nextgenachievers.com. That includes teacher class confirmations, welcome emails, password and username recovery, and the inactivity warning at 11 months. The inactivity warning goes to the account holder, or to the linked Parent account holder for a child account, or to the teacher for a class seat. We do not email a class student or a child on a parent account. The one exception is a password reset for a Child account, which goes to the Parent account holder first and to the child's own email only if we have no parent email.

We use no other third-party services in the app. That means no error monitoring, analytics, third-party fonts or third-party images.

We choose established providers that publish their security and privacy certifications, and we rely on these when using them. We remain responsible for how your information is handled.

## 7. Cookies and browser storage
The app uses a sign-in session cookie, plus a short-lived cookie during password recovery. After a parent enters the parent PIN, a cookie keeps "My child's data" open for 15 minutes. It also stores a list of accounts used on the device, a progress cache and the session in your browser's local storage. We use **no** analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

## 8. Children and schools
The app is designed for children, so we collect as little as we can. In schools, the teacher creates usernames that should not include a child's real name. The school keeps any list that matches usernames to students. Schools and other organizations use the app under their own agreement with us. Outside school, a Parent account holder sets up, or links to and approves, the Child account of a child under 16. Learners aged 16 and 17 can create their own Self sign-up account, and people 18 and over an Adult account. For a Self sign-up account, Launchpad stays locked until a Parent account holder is notified and linked. To link, the Parent account holder agrees to our Terms, including being responsible for that learner's use of NextGenAchievers and Launchpad. Once linked, the Parent account holder is notified when a business is started. There's no approve or decline step for each business. Child accounts already have a linked Parent account holder, so for them this is a notice only. We build privacy into the app by design.

## 9. How long we keep it and how to delete it
We keep information while the account or class is active. An account still waiting for a parent to approve it, or declined, is deleted 30 days after sign-up, together with the whole consent request, including the parent's email address. Emails already delivered, and our email provider's delivery logs, aren't part of our database. If an account has no sign-in or activity at 11 months, we email a warning: to the linked Parent account holder for a child account, to the teacher for a class seat, and to the account holder for a Parent, Self sign-up, Adult or teacher account. We do not email a class student or a child on a parent account. When a warning is sent, deletion waits at least 30 days after that email. Accounts that are still inactive at 12 months are deleted. If there is nobody to email, the account is deleted at 12 months with no warning. Activity records are deleted with the account. We delete our sign-in audit log, which includes IP addresses, after 30 days (see section 2). Hosting and service providers keep short-term technical logs, which may include IP addresses. We don't currently keep restorable backups, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

Deleting an account doesn't cancel a subscription, whether you bought it on our website or through an app store. Please cancel your subscription before you delete your account.

A guest's lessons, coins, Lifetime XP, medals and Vault activity stay on the device. Our server keeps daily counts of guest activity (the day, event type, lesson, app version, device type and country) with no username, email, class, coins, streak, IP address or user agent. Older guest rows that used a made-up username are deleted after 30 days. Clear this device's progress clears the device only.

- In "My child's data", always behind the parent PIN, a parent can view a linked child's data, download it as a plain data file, or delete that child. A parent can also see the parent account record and consent history, and can download or delete that record. Deleting the parent account also deletes every linked child account, and asks for the parent PIN.
- A teacher has a data page for each student, and can export the whole class as one plain data file. A teacher can delete one student or a whole class. This removes those logins, progress and activity records. Deleting the teacher account also removes the class and every student login in it.
- A learner has a read-only My info page. It shows the username and learning track, and who looks after the account: the teacher, or your account manager (the parent or adult who manages your account settings). Learners can't delete their own account in the app, including Self sign-up and Adult account holders. A parent or teacher can delete a learner's account, and anyone can email us to close an account.
- If you cannot use the app, or for any other deletion, email us (see section 12).

## 10. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Teacher passwords and the parent PIN are stored as a hash. Class login passwords are stored encrypted. Access is limited by account role, and only authorized staff can reach stored data. Password reset events record the user ID and role, never the password. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell the people affected, and any authority we're required to tell.

## 11. Access, correction and complaints
You can view, download and delete data in the app (see section 9). You can also email us to see or correct personal information, and we'll respond within 30 days. If you have a privacy complaint, email us first and we will reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can also contact the privacy or data protection authority where you live.

We will post updates here and change the date above. If we make a significant change, such as a new kind of data or a new use of children's information, we will email Parent account holders, Self sign-up account holders, Adult account holders and teachers at the address on their account before it takes effect. Please keep your email address up to date so you don't miss these updates.
